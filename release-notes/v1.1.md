## FlappyBird v1.1（build 1）

首个公开分享的安装包。App 在主屏幕上显示为 **Neon Gate**。

### 包含内容

- 代码绘制的小鸟、云朵、山丘、地面和管道
- 5 档预设难度 + 自定义难度（场景速度 0.85–1.30 倍，管道间距 148–245）
- 10 款皮肤，9 种背景音乐与 9 种音效风格（代码实时合成），各自前 2 款免费
- 本地排行榜与全球榜
- 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / English 运行时切换

### 安装包

| 文件 | 说明 |
| --- | --- |
| `FlappyBird.ipa` | 固定文件名，主页直链使用 |
| `FlappyBird_v1.1_1_adhoc.ipa` | 同一个文件，带版本号 |

- 系统要求：iOS 15.0 或更高，iPhone / iPad，arm64
- 签名：Ad Hoc，**只能安装在描述文件登记过的设备上**；描述文件 2027-04-05 到期
- 未登记设备可尝试用 AltStore、Sideloadly 等工具以自己的 Apple ID 重签安装，不保证可用；内购在此安装包中的可用性未经验证

## FlappyBird v1.1 (build 1)

First publicly shared build. Shows up as **Neon Gate** on the Home Screen.

- Code-drawn bird, clouds, hills, ground and pipes
- 5 difficulty presets + custom difficulty
- 10 skins, 9 music styles and 9 sound effect styles synthesized in code (first 2 of each free)
- Local and global leaderboards
- Live switching between Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English

Requires iOS 15.0 or later (iPhone / iPad, arm64). The IPA is **Ad Hoc signed** and installs only on devices registered in the provisioning profile, which expires on 2027-04-05. Re-signing with AltStore or Sideloadly may work on other devices, with no guarantees. In-app purchases have not been verified in this build.
